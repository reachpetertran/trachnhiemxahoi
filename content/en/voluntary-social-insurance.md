## 1. Who can join

> **Social Insurance Law 2024, Article 2(4)(a):** “Vietnamese citizens aged 15 or over who are not subject to compulsory social insurance and are not receiving a pension, social insurance allowance or monthly allowance”.

For example: former factory workers back in their home village, the self-employed, small traders, farmers, fishers, day labourers.

People whose **employment contract is suspended** (where the two sides have not agreed to keep paying compulsory insurance during that time) can also join (Article 2(4)(b)).

**Note:** if you have an employment contract of 1 month or more, your employer **must** pay compulsory social insurance for you. Voluntary insurance does not replace that duty.

## 2. How much to pay

> **Article 31(2):** “The minimum income used as the basis for voluntary social insurance contributions equals the rural poverty line, and the maximum equals 20 times the reference level at the time of payment.”

> **Article 32(2):** “The voluntary social insurance contribution rate is 22% of the income used as the contribution basis, paid into the retirement and survivorship fund.”

You **choose the income level** you pay on. The higher it is, the more you pay and the higher your pension later.

In 2026 the rural poverty line is still **VND 1,500,000 per person per month**, so:

- **Minimum** contribution: 22% × VND 1,500,000 = **VND 330,000 a month**.
- Example: choose an income of VND 3,000,000 → pay VND 660,000 a month.

## 3. State support

Under Decree 159/2025/NĐ-CP, the State pays a percentage of the **minimum contribution** (based on the rural poverty line), for **up to 10 years** (120 months): <span class="tag chk">Being verified</span>

| Group | Support | 2026: State pays | You pay (minimum) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poor households; people living in island communes or special zones | 50% | VND 165,000 | VND 165,000 |
| Near-poor households | 40% | VND 132,000 | VND 198,000 |
| Ethnic minority people | 30% | VND 99,000 | VND 231,000 |
| Everyone else | 20% | VND 66,000 | VND 264,000 |

The amounts in the table are our own calculation from these rates. If you fit more than one group, you get the highest rate. Some provinces and cities (for example Hà Nội, Hồ Chí Minh City, Đà Nẵng) have resolutions giving **extra support** – ask the social insurance office where you live.

## 4. Pay monthly, quarterly or yearly

Under Article 36(2), you choose one option: **monthly; every 3 months; every 6 months; every 12 months**; a single payment for several years ahead; or a single payment for the years still missing to qualify for a pension.

## 5. What you get

**Pension** – at retirement age with **a full 15 years** or more of contributions (Article 98). Pension amount (Article 99):

- **Women:** 45% of average contribution income for 15 years; each extra year adds 2%, up to 75%.
- **Men:** 15 to under 20 years gives 40%, plus 1% for each extra year; 20 years gives 45%, then 2% for each extra year, up to 75%.

**Maternity allowance – new from 01/07/2025:**

> **Article 95(1):** “The maternity allowance is VND 2,000,000 for each child born and for each foetus of 22 weeks or more that dies in the womb or during labour.”

Condition: you paid voluntary social insurance (or a mix of compulsory and voluntary) **for a full 6 months in the 12 months before the birth**. It covers **women who give birth** and **men whose wives give birth** (Article 94). Submit the papers (copy of the birth certificate or birth notice) to the social insurance agency **within 60 days** of the birth (Articles 96, 97).

**Periods add up:** time you paid compulsory insurance at the factory and time you paid voluntarily both count as social insurance contribution time. If you stop paying before you qualify for a pension, your contribution time is **kept on record** (Article 103).

**Voluntary insurance does not include:** sickness, work accident, occupational disease or unemployment benefits.

## 6. When you can take a lump sum

Under Article 102(1), only if you ask **and** one of these applies:

- you reach retirement age with less than 15 years and do not continue (or you can choose a monthly allowance under Article 23 instead);
- you emigrate to settle abroad;
- you have one of the serious illnesses listed in the law, a work capacity loss of 81% or more, or an especially severe disability;
- you have contribution time **from before 01/07/2025**, have not paid for 12 months, and have less than 20 years in total.

State support is **not paid out** in a lump sum, except in the illness and disability cases (Article 102(3)). **Taking a lump sum means losing that time** when your pension is worked out later.

## 7. Be careful

- Pay only at the **social insurance office** or an **official collection point** in your commune or ward. Get a **receipt**.
- After paying, **check on the VssID app** that the payment has been recorded.
- Watch out for people offering to “get your social insurance paid out early” – they may take your papers and money. The conditions for a lump sum are set by law, as in section 6.

## 8. What you can do

1. **Check** whether you should be on compulsory insurance. If you have a contract of 1 month or more, ask your employer to pay it.
2. **Ask the social insurance office where you live** (or the collection point in your commune or ward) about provincial support and which papers to bring.
3. **Choose an income level and payment schedule** you can keep up. Paying regularly matters more than paying high and then stopping.
4. **Paid at a factory for a few years?** Check your total time on VssID. Keep paying voluntarily to reach 15 years instead of taking a lump sum.
5. **Having a baby:** if you have 6 months of contributions in the 12 months before the birth, claim the VND 2 million allowance within 60 days.
