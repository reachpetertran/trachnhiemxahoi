Know the law, protect yourself

This site is for people in heavy, hazardous or dangerous jobs, for factory workers, and for Vietnamese people working abroad. You will learn what the law says, what to do step by step, and who to call for help. It is completely free. The English version helps auditors, NGOs, employers and anyone supporting Vietnamese workers.

What is happening to you?

  • The company has no contract with me, or never gave me my copy
  • The company keeps my ID card or original diplomas, or asked for a deposit
  • Probation is too long, or probation pay is too low
  • I want to quit – do I have to give notice?
  • Unpaid wages, late pay, unclear payslips
  • Forced overtime, or overtime paid wrongly
  • I have left but have not been paid wages or untaken leave
  • The company does not pay, or underpays, my social insurance

How each article is built

Every article follows the same order, so it is quick to read on a phone:

  1. In short – the three most important points.
  2. What the law says – exact article, clause and document number.
  3. What you can do – concrete steps.
  4. Who to call – checked support contacts.
  5. Checked on and Coming changes.

The Verified label means we checked the information against the original legal text or a government page. Being verified means checking is not finished yet – please confirm before relying on it.
