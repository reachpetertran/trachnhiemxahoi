Children and young people at work

Minimum age, banned work, who protects you

  • Children and young people at work: what each age may do, and who protects youWhat under-13s, 13–14-year-olds and 15–17-year-olds may do and for how many hours; banned work and workplaces (bars, karaoke, building sites, chemicals, offshore fishing…); age-appropriate help at home; call 111 if a child is exploited.
  • Orphans, children without care, children in families in crisis: allowances, school, who protects youOrphans and children whose parents are in prison or missing receive a monthly allowance of VND 810,000–1,350,000; carers get support; state-school pupils pay no tuition; if home has debt, gambling or violence – call 111.
