Farmers and fishers: insurance you can buy yourself, safe pesticide use, safety at sea

Checked on 24/11/2026 · Compiled by Liêm

In short
  • Even without an employment contract you can insure yourself. Voluntary work-accident insurance costs only VND 444,000 a year in 2026, and the State pays 10–30% of it. If you are hurt while doing the work you registered, you get a lump-sum allowance.
  • Pesticides must be used following the “four rights” and you must wait out the pre-harvest interval before harvesting. Use only products on the permitted list. Dispose of empty bottles and bags in the right place.
  • Working for hire on a fishing boat: the boat owner must buy accident insurance for you. You may refuse to sail on an unsafe boat. In an emergency at sea or a natural disaster, call 112 (free).

Quotations of Vietnamese law below are unofficial translations. The Vietnamese text prevails.

1. Insurance: no contract, but you can still protect yourself

Farming, gardening, fish-farming or fishing for your own family is work without an employment contract. The law still gives you the right to insurance.

Law on Occupational Safety and Health 2015, Article 6(3)(c): people working without an employment contract have the right to “take part in and receive voluntary work-accident insurance as regulated by the Government.”

There are three kinds you can join yourself:

Type What it covers Cost in 2026
Voluntary social insurance A pension in old age; a VND 2 million maternity allowance From VND 330,000 a month, State support 20–50% – see separate article
Household health insurance Medical care First person VND 113,850 a month – see health insurance
Voluntary work-accident insurance An allowance if you are injured while working VND 444,000 for 12 months (or VND 222,000 for 6 months)

Health insurance: members of farming, forestry, fishing and salt-making households with an average standard of living get at least 30% of the contribution paid by the State budget (Decree 188/2025/NĐ-CP). Being verified Many provinces add more – ask your commune People's Committee or the social insurance office.

Voluntary work-accident insurance

Few people know this insurance exists. It started on 01/01/2025. Anyone aged 15 or over working without an employment contract can join (Decree 143/2024/NĐ-CP, Article 2).

Decree 143/2024/NĐ-CP, Article 11(3): “a) The 6-month contribution is 6% of the Region IV monthly minimum wage; b) the 12-month contribution is 12% of the Region IV monthly minimum wage.”

In 2026 the Region IV minimum wage is VND 3,700,000. The State pays (Article 12(1)): 30% for poor households, 25% for near-poor households, 10% for everyone else.

You are in 12-month contribution State pays You pay
A poor household VND 444,000 VND 133,200 VND 310,800
A near-poor household VND 444,000 VND 111,000 VND 333,000
Anyone else VND 444,000 VND 44,400 VND 399,600

The amounts are our calculation from Articles 11 and 12.

What do you get after an accident? If a work accident during your cover reduces your working capacity by 5% or more (Article 5), you get a lump-sum allowance (Article 7):

  • 5% reduction: 3 months of the Region IV minimum wage; each extra 1% adds 0.3 of a month.
  • Plus an amount for years of contributions: up to 1 year gives 0.5 of a month; each extra year adds 0.3 of a month.

Hypothetical example: you have paid for 1 year, have an accident and are assessed at 30% reduced capacity. Allowance = (3 + 25 × 0.3) × VND 3,700,000 + 0.5 × VND 3,700,000 = VND 40,700,000 (using the 2026 Region IV minimum wage).

Important: the insurance only pays for accidents that happen while doing the occupation and work, at the times and places you registered (Article 3). When you register, list all the work you really do (for example: rice farming, spraying pesticides, inshore fishing).

After an accident, you or your family must report it straight away to the People's Committee of the commune where it happened (Article 18(2)).

2. Pesticides (insecticides, weedkillers…)

Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine, Article 4(3): “Pesticides must be used according to the four-rights principle: the right product, at the right time, at the right dose and concentration, in the right way; the pre-harvest interval must be observed; use must be effective and safe for people, ensure food safety, minimise environmental pollution and protect ecosystems.”

The pre-harvest interval is the minimum time from the last spraying to harvest (Article 3(22)). The number of days is printed on the label.

Under Article 72, pesticide users:

  • have the right to information and guidance on safe use; to ask the shop that sold the product to explain how to use it according to the label; and to compensation if the shop was at fault;
  • must follow the label instructions; use only products on the List of pesticides permitted in Vietnam; store pesticides safely and collect empty packaging and bottles for disposal at designated places; act at once and report to the commune People's Committee if a pesticide harms people or the environment; and pay compensation for damage caused by wrong use.

Safety tips for spraying (general advice, not law):

  • Wear long sleeves, long trousers, a mask, gloves, goggles and boots.
  • Do not spray into the wind, in strong sun or strong wind.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke while mixing or spraying.
  • Afterwards: wash yourself, and wash spraying clothes separately.
  • Never use pesticide bottles for water or food. Keep pesticides away from children, the kitchen and wells.
  • Do not let anyone under 18 mix or spray pesticides: the law bans employing 15- to 17-year-olds in “producing, using or transporting chemicals” (Labour Code, Article 147(1)(c)).

If someone feels sick, dizzy, short of breath, has fits or faints after contact with pesticides: call 115, move them to fresh air, remove contaminated clothes, and take the bottle or label to the clinic. More on hazard pictograms in Chemicals.

3. Working for hire on a fishing boat

Law on Fisheries 2017, Article 73(5): the boat owner must “buy accident insurance and other compulsory insurance for crew members and people working on the fishing vessel as regulated.”

The owner must also ensure working and living conditions, safety and labour entitlements for the crew (Article 73(3)), and pay the travel costs for you to get home if the captain orders you to leave the boat (Article 73(5)).

Article 74(2): crew members and people working on a fishing vessel have the right “a) to labour entitlements and legitimate rights and interests while working on the vessel under the labour law; b) to refuse to work on a fishing vessel that is not safe”.

Being verified The Law on Fisheries was amended in 2024 and 2025. We have checked the original 2017 text; Articles 73 and 74 have not yet been checked against the latest consolidated text.

You must carry identity papers on board (Article 74(1)). People under 18 are banned from “diving at sea, offshore fishing” (Labour Code, Article 147(1)(g)).

4. Common mistakes

  • Thinking “farmers and fishers can't get insurance”, so every accident or illness is paid out of pocket.
  • Registering for voluntary accident insurance but leaving out work you actually do – then the claim is refused.
  • Buying unlabelled or non-Vietnamese-labelled pesticides; mixing them “stronger” than the label says.
  • Harvesting and selling right after spraying, without waiting out the pre-harvest interval.
  • Throwing empty bottles and bags into ditches and fields.
  • Letting children under 18 spray pesticides or go offshore fishing.
  • Going to sea without asking whether the owner has insured you against accidents.

5. What you can do

  1. Go to the social insurance office or the commune collection point and ask about all three: voluntary social insurance, household health insurance and voluntary work-accident insurance. Ask about extra provincial support.
  2. If money is tight, start with health insurance (illness is common), then accident insurance (just over VND 1,000 a day), then voluntary social insurance.
  3. When registering for accident insurance, list all your occupations, tasks and places of work. Keep the receipt and check it in the VssID app.
  4. Buy pesticides from licensed shops, read the label and ask the seller how to use them. Write down the last spraying date to work out when you may harvest.
  5. Going to sea for hire: ask the owner whether accident insurance has been bought; write down the owner's name, the boat's registration number and a phone number. Tell your family which boat, when you leave and when you expect to return.
  6. Injured while working: get treatment first, then report to the commune People's Committee where it happened and keep all medical papers.

6. Who to call

  • Emergency at sea or on a river; natural disaster, landslide; search and rescue: 112 (free).
  • Pesticide poisoning, accident, serious illness: 115.
  • Tricked onto a boat, papers taken, forced to work, beaten: 113; or 111 (national anti-trafficking line, free).
  • Insurance: the social insurance office where you live, or the commune collection point.
  • Reporting a work accident, asking about support: the commune or ward People's Committee.
  • See also Help contacts.
Coming changes
  • From 01/01/2027, the Region IV minimum wage is expected to be VND 4,040,000 a month (Decree 399/2026/NĐ-CP, full text being checked). Voluntary work-accident insurance for 12 months will then be 12% × VND 4,040,000 = VND 484,800 (our calculation). The minimum voluntary social insurance contribution rises to VND 484,000 a month – see Voluntary social insurance.
  • The Ministry of Health has proposed raising health insurance support for members of households with an average standard of living from 30% to 50%, expected from 01/04/2027. This is a draft, not law yet.
  • Law 21/2026/QH16 amends the Law on Plant Protection and Quarantine, effective 01/01/2027. We have not yet checked every amendment; section 2 will be updated if pesticide users' duties change.
