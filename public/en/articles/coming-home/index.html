Coming home: closing your contract, getting your deposit back, insurance

Checked on 23/11/2026 · Compiled by Liêm

In short
  • Back home, settle (“liquidate”) the placement contract with the sending company within 180 days of the end of your employment contract. Once settled, you get your deposit back with interest.
  • If you have to return early through no fault of your own, the company must refund the service fee for the unworked period, with interest.
  • Register with the residence office within 15 days of arrival. Social insurance paid while abroad counts towards your pension and survivors' benefits.

Quotations of Vietnamese law below are unofficial translations. The Vietnamese text prevails.

1. What the law says

Your obligations on return

Law 69/2020, Article 6(2)(g): the worker must “return home on time after the employment or vocational training contract ends; notify the residence registration office where they lived before going abroad, or of their new residence after returning, under the Law on Residence, within 15 days of entry”.

Staying abroad illegally after the contract ends (without being threatened or forced) is fined VND 80–100 million (Decree 283/2026/NĐ-CP, Article 53(2)).

Settling the contract

Article 46(8): a worker sent by a service company has the right and duty to “settle the placement contract with the service company within 180 days of the end of the employment contract.”

Your deposit

Article 25(3): “The worker shall receive back both the principal and interest of the deposit when the placement contract is settled.”

If you broke the contract, the deposit may only cover damage caused by your fault; anything left must be returned to you (same clause). The amount must be agreed by both sides and written in the settlement document (Decree 112/2021, Article 31(2)).

You get the whole deposit back (principal and interest) when: you complete the contract; you return early without causing damage; you end the contract because of mistreatment, forced labour, threats to your life or health, or sexual harassment; or the company failed to send you on time (Decree 112/2021, Article 32(1)). The bank pays on the basis of the settlement document (clause 2).

Service fee if you return early

Law 69/2020, Article 23(3): where the fee was paid for the whole contract period “and the worker has to return home early through no fault of their own, the service company must refund the service fee and interest in proportion to the remaining period”.

A company that does not refund, or refunds less, is fined VND 80–100 million (Decree 283/2026, Article 49(8)(b)). Not settling, or settling wrongly: VND 10–20 million per worker (Article 49(2)(đ)).

2. Social insurance while working abroad

Workers abroad under contract are covered by compulsory social insurance (Social Insurance Law 2024, Article 2(1)(g)), unless an international treaty provides otherwise.

Article 33(2): “a) Monthly contribution of 22% of the insured wage to the retirement and survivorship fund; b) Paid to the social insurance agency every 3, 6 or 12 months, or in advance in one payment for the period of the overseas contract…”

If you extend or sign a new contract while abroad, you can keep paying, or make back-payments after returning (Article 33(2)(b)). Because contributions go to the retirement and survivorship fund, this period counts towards your pension and survivors' benefits. Check your record on the VssID app – see Social insurance. If you have no job after returning, you can keep paying yourself – see Voluntary social insurance.

3. Support after returning

Under Law 69/2020, Article 67, the Overseas Employment Support Fund helps when you have to return early because of a work accident, sickness or illness that stops you working; the employer's dissolution, bankruptcy or downsizing (disaster, epidemic, war…); or because you ended the contract due to mistreatment, forced labour, threats or sexual harassment. It also helps with disputes and supports families of workers who die or go missing.

Amounts under Decision 34/2025/QĐ-TTg: Being verified

Case Amount
Early return due to accident, sickness or illness VND 10–30 million
Early return due to employer dissolution, bankruptcy or downsizing VND 7–20 million
Early return after ending the contract (mistreatment, forced labour, harassment…) VND 7–20 million

Lawyer and legal advice costs in a dispute 50% of cost, up to VND 50 million per case
Retraining after early return VND 1 million a month, up to 6 months

Condition: you have paid your contribution to the Fund in full.

After returning you also have the right to advice and support to find work or start a business, and access to voluntary psychosocial counselling (Law 69/2020, Article 6(1)(i); Articles 60, 61). Employment service centres provide job information; the sending company helps with job referrals (Article 60(3), (4)).

4. Common problems

  • The company keeps delaying the settlement, so the bank will not release the deposit.
  • “Penalties” deducted from the deposit that are not in the contract and not agreed in writing.
  • Workers who returned early because of mistreatment kept no evidence, so it is hard to prove it was “not their fault”.
  • Workers do not know they paid social insurance while abroad, or lost the receipts.

5. What you can do

  1. Before leaving the host country: get written confirmation that the contract ended, your last payslip, and any papers about accidents or illness. Photograph everything.
  2. Within 15 days of arrival: register with the residence office (commune or ward police) where you live.
  3. Contact the sending company to settle the contract – within 180 days of the end of your employment contract. Ask in writing and keep a copy.
  4. Read the settlement document carefully: deposit returned, service fee refunded, any deductions and why. If you disagree, do not sign.
  5. Take the settlement document to the bank holding your deposit to collect principal and interest.
  6. Check your social insurance on VssID; ask the social insurance office about back-payments if anything is missing.
  7. If you returned early because of an accident, illness, mistreatment or employer bankruptcy: ask the company or the Department of Home Affairs about applying to the Overseas Employment Support Fund.
  8. If the company will not settle or return the deposit: you may petition in writing to the Ministry of Home Affairs (Department of Overseas Labour; before March 2025, the Ministry of Labour) or go to court (Law 69/2020, Article 25(4); Decree 112/2021, Article 32(3)) – see Keep evidence and complain.

6. Who to call

  • The provincial Department of Home Affairs where you live; the Department of Overseas Labour (Ministry of Home Affairs).
  • Free legal aid – see Support contacts.
  • Fraud or trafficking: 111.
Coming changes

Law No. 22/2026/QH16 amends Law 69/2020, effective 01/03/2027: support for returning workers to connect with the domestic job market; no double social insurance or personal income tax where the two countries have an agreement.
