Who do I call?

We only list numbers whose source we have checked. Numbers we could not check are not published.

Last checked: 18/11/2026.

Emergencies in Vietnam

Police

Verified

113

Threats, being held against your will, assault, reporting a crime.

Free call. Merging emergency numbers has been proposed; in November 2026 numbers 113, 114 and 115 still work. Source

Fire and rescue

Verified

114

Fire, explosion, collapse, people trapped.

Free call. Source

Medical emergency

Verified

115

Work accidents, poisoning, fainting, serious injury.

Free call. Source

Search and rescue, incidents, natural disasters

Verified

112

Emergencies at sea or on rivers; floods, landslides, storms; search and rescue.

Free call. In operation under Prime Minister's Decision 2023/QĐ-TTg (2025). Source

National hotline for child protection and anti-trafficking

Verified

111

A child is abused or made to work; a person is tricked, sold or forced to work (in Vietnam or abroad).

24/7, free. Since 1 July 2025, 111 is also the national line for reporting human trafficking (Decree 162/2025/NĐ-CP, Article 4). Source

National domestic violence hotline

Verified

117

Beaten, verbally abused, money controlled, forced sex, thrown out of home… – report, get advice and referral to local support.

24/7, free. Activated by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism on 8 September 2026, under the 2022 Law on Domestic Violence Prevention and Control and Decree 76/2023/NĐ-CP. Source

Legal help and complaints in Vietnam

State Legal Aid Centre (each province or city)

Verified

Free lawyers or legal aid officers for eligible people (poor households, children, people with disabilities in financial hardship, trafficking victims, victims of domestic violence…).

Under the provincial Department of Justice. From 1 January 2027 eligibility widens to near-poor households, people with disabilities and some low-income workers (Law 05/2026/QH16). Workers in ordinary labour disputes are not automatically eligible. Source

Provincial Department of Home Affairs; commune or ward People's Committee

Verified

Where to report problems with wages, contracts and workplace safety; appoints labour mediators.

Since 1 July 2025, tasks of the former district labour offices moved to the provincial Department of Home Affairs and commune-level People's Committees (Decree 129/2025/NĐ-CP). Source

Taiwan

1955 Hotline (Taiwan Ministry of Labor)

Verified

1955

Labour law advice, disputes, abuse, violence, suspected trafficking; interpreting available.

24 hours, 365 days, free, Vietnamese available. Source

Japan

OTIT – Vietnamese-language advice for technical intern trainees

Verified

0120-250-168

Unpaid wages, forced overtime, violence, wanting to change workplace.

Free. Sources give different opening hours – check the latest on otit.go.jp. Source

Foreign Residents Support line (Immigration, Japan Ministry of Justice)

Verified

0570-013904

Residence cards, visas, immigration procedures.

Vietnamese available. Number printed in the trainee handbook published by OTIT. Source

Advice line for foreign workers (Japan Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare)

Verified

0570-001706

Working conditions, wages.

Vietnamese available; call charges apply. Opening hours being checked. Number printed in the trainee handbook published by OTIT. Source
